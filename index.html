Do this Sunday afternoon and 80% of your week is already assembled. Work in parallel — oven + stove + counter all going at once.

Your Nutrition Profile

  • Goal: Weight loss on Wegovy while trying to conceive (TTC)
  • Priority: High protein (~115g/day) to preserve lean mass
  • Allergies: Shrimp, crab (no shellfish anywhere in this plan)

TTC Pregnancy-Safe Guardrails

  • ✅ Wild salmon, cod (low-mercury, high DHA for baby's brain)
  • ✅ Eggs daily for choline
  • ✅ Lentils, beans, dark leafy greens for folate & iron
  • ✅ Full-fat dairy — linked to better fertility outcomes
  • ❌ No swordfish, king mackerel, tilefish, marlin, bigeye tuna (high mercury)
  • ❌ No raw fish, raw eggs, unpasteurized dairy
  • ⚠️ Deli turkey (Thursday wrap): heat until steaming to kill listeria
  • ⚠️ Keep caffeine under 200 mg/day (~1 medium coffee)

Wegovy Pro Tips

  • Portion small — with reduced appetite, half a serving may fill you. Store the rest.
  • Sip liquids AWAY from meals — 30 min before/after reduces nausea.
  • Protein first, always — eat the protein portion first before carbs/fats.
  • Cold foods = easier on rough days — chia pudding, salmon salad, overnight oats.
  • Ginger tea in the pantry — anti-inflammatory AND settles Wegovy nausea.

Anti-Inflammatory Backbone

  • Mediterranean-style: olive oil, fatty fish 2–3x/week, berries daily
  • Leafy greens daily · Turmeric + ginger in spice rotation
  • Nuts/seeds for omega-3s · Minimal refined sugar & ultra-processed foods
